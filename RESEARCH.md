## Particularly close additional work

[Therianos, frozen larval rate operator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17745v2), submitted 16 Jun and revised 2 Jul 2026, compares a frozen leaky-tanh larval connectome with degree-and-weight rewiring. Gross dynamics largely follow coarse statistics; spatial routing can retain topology effects. This is a close methods precedent, not an LLM regulator replication. Article is CC BY 4.0. Public [verification bundle](https://github.com/Stavros963/fcta) is identified; full analysis pipeline was described as available on request until publication. We do not claim to reproduce its numerical results. Our extension tests a frozen LLM's objective task return and causal use of controller output, with the complete supplied matrix rather than a selected strongly connected core.

Older precedents include [imposed connectome ESN topology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.09359), [connectome features for ESNs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02094), and [lateral-horn reservoir multifunctionality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01885). These establish that the general connectome-reservoir idea is not new; they were identified in searches, not numerically reproduced. Their code/data licences were not checked because no artifacts are reused.

## Data actually used

[Winding et al., The connectome of an insect brain, Science 2023](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.add9330), Supplementary Data S1, from the [brain-networks research mirror](https://github.com/brain-networks/larval-drosophila-connectome) at immutable commit `15e065f5c29f08c96ccd64ea8fe0f51510629009`. The primary publisher page returned HTTP 403 to our browser. The mirror attributes the archive to Winding; [Cambridge primary repository](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/5a5f1f5e-5efe-45e4-bd93-051d669215ac) verifies the paper and lists CC BY 4.0 for its preprint. The mirror has no separate data licence: that preprint licence is NOT asserted to license the archive. Local research use only; no redistribution rights inferred. This provenance/licensing ambiguity is retained as a limitation, not silently resolved by the mirror's public availability.

We use all 2,952 rows of `all-all_connectivity_matrix.csv`, 110,677 directed pairs, 352,611 counted contacts, including 537 self-edges. This is an intact supplied matrix, not the final paper's full 3,016-neuron / ~548k-synapse headline graph, not an adult fly brain or entire CNS. The difference is inherited from the release; no artificial neurons, invented edges, thresholding or core selection are added. Archive SHA-256 `8c1f43809ed5d527ba61b154e377cc21da26383a75eda8aab85ce05607a72a4c`; numeric CSV and derived hashes in `data/manifest.json`. Rows are interpreted as presynaptic and columns postsynaptic, checked against compartment matrices and input/output metadata in the harness audit.
